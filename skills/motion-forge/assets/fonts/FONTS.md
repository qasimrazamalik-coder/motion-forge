licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 — free to embed in any composition or video.

| Inter-Regular.woff | Inter | same | OFL-1.1 |
| Lora-Variable.woff, Lora-Italic-Variable.woff | Lora (wght 400–700) | © 2011 The Lora Project Authors (github.com/cyrealtype/Lora-Cyrillic) | OFL-1.1 |

Use `fonts.css` (copied into projects by `scaffold.mjs`). Prefer project-supplied brand fonts when a
brief provides them; check their license before embedding.
